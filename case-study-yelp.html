Case study · Yelp

Reframing local intent and conversion opportunity with customer clustering.

Local demand Customer intent Offer strategy Discovery

Business question

How could local demand and customer behavior be understood in a way that improved discovery, conversion, and offer relevance for a broad set of categories and intents?

Data / research

  • Search, discovery, and transactional behavior across local categories
  • Spatial and timing patterns influencing when intent converts to action
  • Customer segmentation based on local demand and category opportunity

The opportunity was not about more impressions — it was about better matching intent to the right local offer, message, and timing.

Analysis

By clustering customer journeys at a local level, the work surfaced clear patterns: some users were habit-driven, others were highly transaction-dependent, and some were opportunity-driven but not yet ready to convert. These differences had major implications for targeting and offer design.

Key insight

  • Local intent varied sharply by time, category, and customer lifecycle stage
  • Strong demand existed beyond a single “top category” lens, but it required better clustering
  • Offer strategy performed best when aligned with the user’s next likely action, not just broad location-based demand

Recommendation

  • Prioritize segmented local offerings instead of uniform city-level campaigns
  • Use behavioral clustering to identify where discovery can be improved and conversion can be increased
  • Turn local demand patterns into actionable partner and campaign strategy
