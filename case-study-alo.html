Case study · Alo

Mapping demand signals to customer segments and growth opportunity.

Brand strategy Segmentation Demand analysis Marketing planning

Business question

How could Alo better identify which customer groups were most likely to respond to acquisition, retention, and category expansion efforts? The goal was to move from broad brand appeal to sharper segmentation and channel strategy.

Data / research

  • Customer and transaction data across purchase behavior and activation moments
  • Category and demand analysis across local patterns and purchase timing
  • Competitive and market research to frame brand position in relation to adjacent segments

The key was not just “who buys,” but “which cohorts are becoming repeat customers, and which messages best convert interest into actual demand.”

Analysis

The work clustered customer behavior into a few repeatable patterns: occasional category buyers, high-intent lifestyle shoppers, and value-oriented discovery customers. Each group differed in offer sensitivity, product affinities, and likely channel response.

Key insight

  • Category expansion only works when the audience segment is already behaviorally aligned
  • Customer intent was stronger when the brand message reflected a specific need state rather than broad lifestyle positioning
  • Timing and context mattered as much as product mix in determining conversion

Recommendation

  • Build segment-based acquisition and retention journeys tailored to customer behavior and category affinity
  • Shift creative and offer strategy toward high-intent groups instead of broad audience campaigns
  • Use customer signal to guide category expansion and brand storytelling decisions
