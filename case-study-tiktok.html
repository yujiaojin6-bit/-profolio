Case study · TikTok

Turning content behavior into an actionable purchase-intent narrative.

Platform strategy Creative testing Audience signal Campaign planning

Business question

How could campaign and product teams understand whether short-form creative was truly driving demand, and which audience segments were most likely to convert after exposure?

Data / research

  • Platform engagement patterns, content format, and audience response data
  • Creative testing and message resonance analysis across customer cohorts
  • Performance and funnel analysis linked to demand, click-through, and conversion

The strongest learning was that content resonance was not enough on its own — the real opportunity was pairing the right message with the right audience state.

Analysis

The work compared content themes, audience composition, and conversion patterns to isolate what actually moved intent. The result was a sharper view of which creative styles were creating demand and which were rewarding existing awareness rather than driving action.

Key insight

  • Demand was most likely to scale when creative matched specific audience motivations
  • Message repetition created awareness but had weaker conversion lift unless paired with a clear offer or category signal
  • Creative testing needed to be tied to conversion thresholds, not only engagement metrics

Recommendation

  • Reallocate budget toward creative variants that align message to intent, rather than broad engagement wins
  • Shift from awareness-led content to conversion-focused creative frameworks
  • Integrate audience and message strategy into campaign planning for scalable demand generation
