03 / 04 · TIKTOK

TikTok

A reach-prediction study testing which creator and video signals move views.

Capability demonstrated: Engagement and reach prediction with regression and machine learning.

12,344videos analyzed
View Presentation ↗
Question
Which creator and content signals predict video views?
What I did
  • Analyzed creator, visual, facial-expression, transcript, and topic features.
  • Log-transformed views, likes, shares, and comments; fit OLS baselines.
  • Compared Ridge/Lasso stability, then Random Forest for nonlinear view patterns.
  • Compared out-of-sample R² and variable importance.
Result
Random Forest explained more view variance than OLS; creator scale led variable importance.
Recommendation
Prioritize creator scale and calm or surprise emotion; do not rely on topic tags for reach.
04 / 04 · YELP

Yelp

A restaurant analysis linking reviewer credibility, pricing, and location to performance.

Capability demonstrated: Restaurant performance analytics with rating and survival models.

View Presentation ↗
Question
What shapes restaurant ratings and survival?
What I did
  • Modeled ratings with Elite-reviewer, price, age, location, and review-volume data.
  • Compared nested rating regressions with F-tests.
  • Modeled business survival with logistic regression and odds ratios.
  • Compared survival models using likelihood-ratio tests, AIC, and BIC.
Result
The selected survival model combined four predictors and had the best information-criterion fit.
Recommendation
Assess review volume, price, business age, and reviewer credibility together.
