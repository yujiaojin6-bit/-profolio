Caribou Coffee

Loyalty Analytics & Growth Strategy
Data Analytics Customer Insight Growth Strategy CRM Campaigns

How can Caribou increase visit frequency among Casual Loyalists?

A client consulting project based on a loyalty database of more than two million members, focused on identifying the behaviors and marketing opportunities that could increase customer visit frequency.

Business Question

How could Caribou identify the behaviors most likely to increase loyalty engagement and generate two additional visits per Casual Loyalist each year?

Methodology & Tools
2M+ Member Database SQL R Descriptive Analytics Propensity Score Matching Difference-in-Differences Secondary Research Survey Research
My Impact

I led the overall data analysis and the App & Order Ahead workstream, uncovering behavioral patterns and testing whether digital ordering adoption contributed to higher visit frequency. I then helped translate the combined findings into three strategic growth themes and six execution-ready campaign action plans.

Verified Evidence

3 strategic themes
6 campaign plans
