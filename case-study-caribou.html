Case study · Caribou Coffee

Rebuilding customer retention and visit growth for a 2M+ member loyalty base.

Business question Research Segmentation CRM targeting

Business question

Caribou had a large loyalty base, but a fragmented understanding of what actually drove repeat visits. The challenge was to identify which members were most likely to respond to offers, how value varied by visit frequency, and which customer segments should be prioritized for retention and CRM targeting.

Data / research

  • 2M+ loyalty member records across purchase, channel, and recency patterns
  • PSM + DID framework to isolate behavioral drivers and demand sensitivity
  • Offer and campaign response analysis across traffic, conversion, and visitation
  • Customer interviews and category-level research to validate hypothesis

The primary insight was not simply “who spends more,” but “which members were most likely to convert from an offer into a repeat visitation pattern.” That distinction drove the strategic recommendation.

Analysis

2M+ member records analyzed
25% incremental monthly visit lift
10% conversion lift

Key insight

High-frequency members were not the only strategic opportunity. The most scalable growth came from a second tier of lapsed but still engaged members: they were highly responsive to relevant offers, active across channels, and more likely to return when the message matched their behavior and purchase rhythm.

  • Member value differed meaningfully by lifecycle stage, not just basket size
  • Offer responsiveness tracked closely with recency, frequency, and channel mix
  • Segment-level targeting was materially more effective than broad discounting

Recommendation

  • Prioritize a layered CRM strategy: reactivation for at-risk members, personalized retention for high-value members, and conversion-based offers for recently lapsed segments
  • Shift from generic promotions to offer targeting based on recency, visit cadence, and favored channel behavior
  • Build campaign concepts around “visit behavior recovery” rather than one-time discounting

Business impact

The work translated analytical insight into a practical growth plan that could be pitched to leadership. It moved the decision from “what are the members buying?” to “what should we do next for each segment?”

  • Improved targeting efficiency across CRM campaigns
  • Created a clearer customer segmentation framework for future lifecycle strategy
  • Enabled executive alignment around a measurable, repeatable growth lever
